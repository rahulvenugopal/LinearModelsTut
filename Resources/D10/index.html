Day 10

When Your Predictor Disappears

Decompose causal pathways and understand when predictors disappear using mediation analysis.

Topics Covered

Coefficient Collapse

Understand why significant predictors can suddenly vanish in a multiple regression when adding new variables.

Mediation Analysis

Decompose the total effect into direct and indirect pathways using the mediation framework.

Partial Regression

Visualize the unique variance of predictors after removing the linear influence of other variables.

Key Concepts

  • Baron & Kenny's framework for causal mediation
  • Direct vs. Indirect effects (ACME and ADE)
  • Bootstrapping for significance testing of mediated paths
  • Full vs. Partial mediation

Homework

  • Fit a multiple regression and observe coefficient collapse
  • Perform a formal mediation analysis using the mediation package
  • Create path diagrams and partial regression plots to visualize your findings
