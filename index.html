One Model to Rule Them All

Most statistics courses present t‑tests, correlations, ANOVA, and regression as unrelated tools. This course reveals the deeper structure: they are all the same model. Understanding the linear model framework makes advanced neuroscience analysis efficient including mixed models.

Course Roadmap

Day 1

The R Awakening

Escape the spreadsheet matrix. We ditch Excel and GraphPad to tour RStudio, install packages, and build a reproducible foundation.

Day 2

Painting with Penguins

Meet the Palmer Penguins. Dive straight into data visualization using ggplot2 and Hadley's verbs to wrangle and plot beautifully.

Day 3

The Meanest Benchmark

Stop memorizing flowcharts! We introduce linear models using the mean as our baseline, graduating to simple regression (one X, one Y).

Day 4

The Great Statistical Hoax

The veil is lifted. We prove that correlations, t-tests, and ANOVAs are actually just simple regression wearing different hats.

Day 5

Size Matters

Beyond p-values. A theory dive into effect sizes and mind-bending statistical quirks, like paired vs. unpaired tests on the same data.

Day 6

The Covariate Plot Twist

Life isn't simple, and neither is data. Learn how to add covariates into your regression models to control for confounding variables.

Day 7

Bulletproofing Your Data

What happens when assumptions fail? Explore robust statistics to handle outliers and heavy tails without throwing away precious data.

Day 8

When Data Gets Needy

Neuroscience data is messy and hierarchical. Discover why standard regression fails here and why we desperately need mixed-effects models.

Day 9

Mixing It Up

The main event. We build linear mixed models (LMMs), separating fixed effects from random intercepts and slopes for complex designs.

Day 10

The Grand Unification

Tying it all together. A comprehensive summary of the unified modeling framework, followed by an open QnA for your specific data problems.

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

  • Command Reproducible Workflows: Confidently navigate RStudio, wrangle messy datasets using dplyr verbs, and generate publication-ready plots using ggplot2 instead of relying on fragile spreadsheets.
  • See the Matrix of Linear Models: Stop memorizing fragmented decision trees. You will understand how to build and interpret simple and multiple regressions, recognizing that t-tests, ANOVAs, and correlations are all just special cases of the same core model.
  • Control for the Real World: Successfully integrate covariates to control for confounding variables and apply robust statistical methods to handle outliers and heavy-tailed data without artificially trimming your datasets.
  • Master Hierarchical Data (LMMs): Identify when standard regression fails and successfully deploy Linear Mixed Models (LMMs) to properly analyze the nested, hierarchical, or repeated-measures data common in neuroscience.
Designed for neuroscientists transitioning to modern reproducible data analysis.
